Our works

We take on MES contracts in three areas of civil and electrical construction.

Roads

Construction, repair and resurfacing of roads inside military stations and cantonments.

Buildings

New construction, renovation and maintenance of barracks, offices, residences and other service buildings.

Electrical

Internal and external electrical installation, wiring, lighting and maintenance works.

About us

M/S Chander Muni is a family-run contracting firm registered to work with the Military Engineer Services. Over three decades we have built a record of finishing government works properly and on schedule.

The firm is led by its founder, Sanjeev Kumar, and co-founder, Manish Kumar, who are brothers. Both take part in the work, from bidding on tenders to inspecting finished sites.
